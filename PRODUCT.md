# Product

**Kioku Tower** (記憶, "memory") is a browser memory-card roguelike made by an older brother for his sister Jenny (part of "Jenny's World"). Free, non-commercial, hosted on GitHub Pages.

- **Core loop:** every card in the deck appears twice face-down; flipping a pair costs energy; a match plays the card. Climb three floors of the "Net Café Tower" on a branching map (fights, elites, events, shops, rest booths, treasure, bosses), building a deck with play styles (Glitch, Guard, Combo, Memory) and collecting charms.
- **Audience and scene:** Jenny, casually, on a phone or a laptop, short sessions, often in the evening.
- **Surface mode:** Operate/Experience hybrid: a game UI. Readability of cards, energy, HP and enemy intent beats decoration.
- **Brand commitments (pinned by the user):**
  - Flat chibi vector look: thick even outlines, flat colour, one shine highlight, big-head/tiny-body characters, blob backgrounds with rings and sparkles (reference: a flat chibi sticker illustration on a deep red ground).
  - Jenny is an original character (cat-ear hood, purple hair with lilac tips, pink crescent-moon pin). Default look "Sparkle Eyes"; "Happy Classic" and "Lilac Wink" selectable in Settings.
  - Music "Her Shiba Are Her Knights" (default 25%), generated sound effects (default 40%).
- **Never:** copyrighted characters; anything that slows play down.
