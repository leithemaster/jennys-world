## Rules for the styles

- **Sleepy → Asleep:** an enemy that reaches 3 Sleepy falls Asleep and skips its next 2 turns (bosses 1). Its Sleepy resets, it can't get more while asleep, and each time it wakes up it takes 3 more Sleepy to fall asleep again.
- **Cozy:** on a Cozy card, healing past your max HP hits the enemy for the extra amount.
- **Exposed:** the enemy takes 50% more damage. Wears off by 1 each enemy turn.
- **No card draw:** anything that would "draw a card" reveals cards instead.
- **Hybrid cards** have two styles and count toward both, so mixed decks keep finding cards for either half.
- **Conditional cards** stay out of rewards until your deck can use them (for example Roast needs an Exposed card). Starter cards count for conditions but never pick your deck's style.
